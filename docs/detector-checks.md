# Detector checks on real candles

Source: binance, closed candles only. Generated by `scripts/check_signals.py`. Each example's numbers are recomputed from the raw candles; open the same coin, timeframe and candle time on any chart to compare.

## Tuning notes (2026-10-20)

First run on 150 coins gave 159 new signals. Looking at the evidence below, three rules were tightened:

- **Structure break**: a BTC 1h "bearish break" closed 0.01% below the swing low, which is noise. The close must now
  clear the level by 0.2 ATR.
- **RSI**: RSI hovering around 30 fired several times in a row (1h oversold 2.6 per 100 candles). It now fires again
  only after leaving the zone by 5 points (2.1 per 100).
- **Double top / bottom**: on 1h, peaks within 3% were often two unrelated highs above a shallow dip (2.9 per 100
  candles). The two peaks must now be closer to each other than half the dip between them (2.0 per 100), on top of
  the 3% rule and a dip of at least 1.5 ATR.
